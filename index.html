Welcome to the Flower Mound Pollinator Garden

The Flower Mound Pollinator Garden is a community lead project to grow native plants to provide habitat for local pollinator insects and to educate our community on how they can do the same.

About the Garden

The Pollinator Garden is focused on growing native flowering species, creating habitat for our local pollinator species like bees, butterflies, hummingbirds and more! Some of the species you can expect to find are:

  • Milkweed
  • Red Yucca
  • Firewheel
  • Sunflowers
  • Beebalms
  • Vervains

Our Goal

Our aim is for the garden to serve as a sanctuary for pollinators and as an accessible, educational environment for local community members. We want to bring our community together and promote consciousness and appreciation for local pollinator species and the native plants that support them. We also want to promote eco-minded gardening practices and help promote xeriscaping, or the practice of landscaping with plants that are drought tolerant and require less water.
